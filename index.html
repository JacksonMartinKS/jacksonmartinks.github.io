      How the forecasts work

      NFL. Every team carries an Elo rating with a separate quarterback adjustment. The rest of the season is played out 100,000 times to get each team's range of records, playoff seeds, Super Bowl odds, and draft position.

      Kansas House. Each of the 125 districts gets a win probability, and the full election is simulated with a shared statewide swing to show how many seats each party could end up with, and how likely Democrats are to break the Republican supermajority.

      These are probabilities, not predictions. A 70% favorite should still lose about three times in ten.
