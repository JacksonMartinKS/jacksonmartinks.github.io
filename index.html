      How it works

      NFL. Every team carries an Elo rating with a separate quarterback adjustment. The rest of the season is played out 100,000 times to get each team's range of records, playoff seeds, Super Bowl odds, and draft position.

      Kansas. Each of the 125 House districts gets a win probability, and the full election is simulated with a shared statewide swing to get the seat totals. The statewide and U.S. House races blend a fundamentals estimate with a polling average.

      Fair maps. Proposed districts for every state, scored on past presidential and House results and compared with the lines actually in use.

      Forecasts are probabilities, not predictions. A 70% favorite should still lose about three times in ten.
